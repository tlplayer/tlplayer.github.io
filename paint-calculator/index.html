Paint planning

  • Keep one labeled can for touch-ups.
  • Porous walls may need extra paint.
  • Calculate ceilings and trim separately.

How much paint do I need?

Find wall area from the room perimeter and height, subtract door and window area, multiply by the number of coats, then divide by the paint's labeled coverage.

Gallons = ((room perimeter × height) − openings) × coats ÷ coverage

Paint coverage per gallon

Many interior paints cover roughly 350 to 400 square feet per gallon on a smooth, sealed surface. Bare drywall, masonry, texture, dramatic color changes, and application technique can reduce coverage.

Do I need two coats?

Two finish coats commonly provide even color and durable coverage. A similar-color repaint with a high-quality product may need less; new surfaces usually need an appropriate primer plus finish coats.
