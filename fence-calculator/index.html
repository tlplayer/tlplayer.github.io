Fence Calculator

Estimate the posts, panels, sections, and concrete bags for a straight fence run.

Layout checklist

  • Mark property lines and utilities.
  • Count corner posts separately.
  • Check local frost depth and code.

How many fence posts do I need?

Divide the usable fence length by the maximum post spacing, round up to find sections, then add an end post. This estimate also adds a second gate post when a gate is entered.

Posts = round up(fence length ÷ post spacing) + 1

Typical fence post spacing

Many prefabricated wood panels are eight feet wide, so posts are set to match the actual panel width. Wind loads, terrain, material, and local requirements can call for closer spacing.

Concrete per fence post

Concrete needs depend on post diameter, hole width, and hole depth. Two 50-pound bags is a useful placeholder for many light residential posts, but deep frost lines and large gate posts can require considerably more.
