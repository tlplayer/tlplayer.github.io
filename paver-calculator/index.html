Paver Calculator

Estimate the number of rectangular pavers needed for a patio, walkway, or other paved area.

Ordering tips

  • Check actual paver dimensions.
  • Add more waste for curves and diagonals.
  • Order one dye lot when color matters.

How many pavers do I need?

Divide the total project area by the face area of one paver, then add an allowance for cuts, breakage, and future repairs.

Pavers = project area ÷ paver face area × (1 + waste percentage)

How much extra should I order?

Ten percent is a common allowance for a simple running bond in a rectangular area. Curves, angled cuts, mixed sizes, or complex patterns can require 15% or more.

Remember the base materials

Pavers are only the finish layer. Most projects also need compacted aggregate base, bedding sand, edge restraint, and joint sand. Base depth depends on soil, climate, and expected load.
