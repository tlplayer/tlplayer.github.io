Sod Calculator

Estimate sod rolls, pallets, order coverage, and optional material cost for a rectangular lawn.

Use your supplier's quoted roll coverage

Before ordering

  • Confirm roll and pallet coverage locally.
  • Measure each lawn section separately.
  • Plan delivery close to installation time.

How much sod do I need?

Multiply lawn length by width, add an allowance for trimming and irregular edges, then divide by the supplier's roll or pallet coverage.

Sod rolls = lawn area × (1 + extra percentage) ÷ coverage per roll

How much extra sod should I order?

Five percent is a practical starting point for a simple rectangle. Curves, narrow strips, diagonal cuts, and irregular borders can require more.

Measure multiple lawn areas

Break an irregular yard into rectangles, calculate each section, and add the areas. Keep beds, patios, and other sections that will not receive sod out of the measurements.

Supplier coverage matters

Roll dimensions and pallet quantities vary by grass type, grower, and region. Replace the defaults with the coverage stated by the actual supplier before purchasing.
