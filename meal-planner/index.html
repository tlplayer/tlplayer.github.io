How the planner chooses meals

A deterministic search balances your calorie, protein, time and ingredient-cost preferences while favoring ingredient reuse. It keeps pinned meals. It is a small recipe library, not a guarantee that every combination can meet every target. Plan notes show misses, including package costs and carbohydrate thresholds.

Low-carb uses a 130 g/day total-carbohydrate planning threshold; keto style uses 50 g/day net carbohydrate (carbs minus fiber). These are this tool’s labels, not medical recommendations or a promise of ketosis. Calorie and protein targets apply per person; the same menu and portion size are used for each household member.

Nutrition, price & cooking estimates

Nutrition is calculated from the representative ingredient estimates in this site’s dataset. It has not been laboratory-tested and is not an official restaurant or USDA nutrition record. Brand, preparation, portion size and retained cooking oil change the result. Check your package labels; USDA FoodData Central is a reference for looking up ingredients.

Weights are edible amounts in the stated raw, dry or drained form. Package sizes for canned foods use drained yield. Recipe cost is ingredient cost consumed; checkout cost buys whole packages. Times assume a small batch and do not include overnight chilling. Prep and cooking times are added across the day without assuming meal-prep savings.

METs, activity energy & percentages

Gross kcal/min = MET × 3.5 × body weight in kg ÷ 200. Net kcal/min uses max(MET − 1, 0) instead. Equivalent minutes = meal kcal ÷ kcal/min. Meal energy percentage = estimated activity kcal ÷ meal kcal × 100. Values can exceed 100%; the ring fills to 100% while the label shows the full estimate.

Activity values come from the 2024 Compendium of Physical Activities. Estimates vary with age, fitness, movement efficiency and other factors. Choose activity suited to your ability. These calculations are context, not instructions to compensate for eating.

Cooking safely & checking ingredients

Use a food thermometer: chicken to 165°F / 74°C, ground beef to 160°F / 71°C, and fish to 145°F / 63°C. Cook eggs until whites and yolks are firm. Follow the FoodSafety.gov temperature chart. Cooking times never replace a temperature check.

Allergen notes flag common ingredients, but do not assess manufacturing cross-contact or every possible allergen. Check every product label.

Saving your plan, privacy & advertising

Your meal plan, body weight and store quotes are processed in this tab. Our planner does not upload them or save them automatically. Recipe views, plan additions, shopping-list additions and order-link clicks are recorded in a temporary event log in this tab. No analytics destination is connected; these events are not sent to a server. The log excludes body weight, calorie targets, prices and identity. Outbound restaurant and delivery links open external sites and do not place an order. Use Save plan to download a JSON file and Open plan to restore it; export CSVs for your menu, activities and shopping list. Refreshing or closing the tab clears the workspace.

This site loads Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and technical information to serve and measure ads, including personalized advertising based on previous visits. Manage choices in Google Ads Settings or AdChoices. Read Google’s information about partner sites. Store searches send the ingredient search term to the selected retailer under that retailer’s privacy policy.
