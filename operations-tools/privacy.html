Your files and calculations

The tools process imported CSV files and manually entered data in your browser. Our calculator code does not upload that data to a server or save it in persistent browser storage. You can manage or opt out of personalized Google advertising in Google Ads Settings, and find choices for some other advertising providers at AdChoices.
