About

Useful estimates without the friction.

BuildEstimate is a collection of free, browser-based calculators and planning tools for construction, landscaping, and home improvement. Calculator math, project lists, local terrain files, and 3D models are processed on your device.

How to use these estimates

Material dimensions, density, installation methods, local building codes, and site conditions vary. Use each result as a conceptual planning estimate, confirm package coverage with your supplier, and consult qualified professionals when structural, grading, drainage, code, or safety decisions are involved.

Who made this?

BuildEstimate is an independent project by Timothy Player.
