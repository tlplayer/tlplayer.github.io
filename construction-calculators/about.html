About

Useful estimates without the friction.

BuildEstimate is a collection of free, browser-based calculators for construction, landscaping, and home improvement planning. Every result is calculated on your device. There are no accounts, uploads, or stored project measurements.

How to use these estimates

Material dimensions, density, installation methods, local building codes, and site conditions vary. Use each result as a planning estimate, confirm package coverage with your supplier, and consult a qualified professional when structural or safety decisions are involved.
