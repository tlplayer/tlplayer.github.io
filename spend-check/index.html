CSV fields & bookkeeping conventions

Required columns: date, vendor, description, quantity, unit_price. Download the template for optional invoice, PO, SKU, category, cost center, GL account, tax, shipping, paid, due date, supplier flags, and entry type fields.

Use YYYY-MM-DD dates, plain decimal amounts, and true/false flags. Each row is one invoice line. Allocate tax, shipping and payments to each line once; do not repeat invoice totals on every line. Only purchases count toward spend and balances. Estimates are separate. Credits and currency conversion are not supported.

Data stays in memory for this tab and is lost on refresh or close. Export your CSV to save your work. No analytics, remote scripts, or storage are used on this page.
