Measure before buying

  • Measure openings at the top, middle, and bottom.
  • Include trim, hinges, handles, and hookups.
  • Check every doorway, turn, stair, and elevator.

How to measure for a refrigerator

Measure the cabinet opening at its narrowest width and height, then measure usable depth to the wall. Check the product specification sheet for ventilation, hinge, handle, door-swing, water-line, and electrical-clearance requirements.

How to measure for a dishwasher

Measure between the cabinets at the top and bottom, from the finished floor to the underside of the counter, and from the back wall to the cabinet face. Account for flooring installed in front of an older unit, which can trap it beneath the counter.

Ranges, washers, dryers, and freezers

Confirm the finished opening, hookup locations, door or lid swing, ventilation, and service access. Laundry appliances may also require room for hoses, ducts, stacking hardware, or pedestals.

Delivery-path measurement

Measure the narrowest clear width with doors open or removed. Compare turns and landings as well as straight doorways. This tool provides a basic width check; product packaging and rotation can require more space.

Required opening = appliance dimensions + manufacturer clearances
