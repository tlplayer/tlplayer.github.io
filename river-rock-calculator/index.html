Coverage tips

  • Use a depth near twice the average rock size.
  • Install edging before measuring.
  • Add extra for curves and low areas.

How much river rock do I need?

River rock coverage depends on area and installed depth. Multiply length, width, and depth, then convert the resulting cubic feet to cubic yards.

Cubic yards = length × width × (depth in inches ÷ 12) ÷ 27

Choosing river rock depth

A practical rule is to install rock at roughly twice its average diameter so the landscape fabric is covered. Smaller stone may cover well at two inches; larger cobbles can require four inches or more.

River rock weight

Decorative stone commonly weighs around 1.3 to 1.5 tons per cubic yard, but rock type, grading, and moisture matter. The calculator lets you replace the default with your supplier's figure.
