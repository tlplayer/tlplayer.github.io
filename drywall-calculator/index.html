Installation notes

  • Plan sheet orientation before ordering.
  • Use moisture-rated board where required.
  • Check local fire-rating requirements.

How many sheets of drywall do I need?

Add the area of all four walls and, if needed, the ceiling. Add waste, divide by the coverage of one sheet, and round up.

Sheets = total surface area × (1 + waste percentage) ÷ sheet area

Should I subtract doors and windows?

For a typical room, leaving openings in the area creates a useful allowance for cuts and damaged pieces. Large openings can be deducted when they would meaningfully change the order.

Drywall screws and joint compound

This estimator uses 32 screws per sheet and about 0.053 pounds of ready-mix compound per square foot as rough planning figures. Framing spacing, board thickness, finish level, and installer technique will change actual usage.
