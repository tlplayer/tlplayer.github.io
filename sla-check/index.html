CSV · up to 10 MB / 10,000 rows · ISO timestamps

Import replaces the ticket list after validation. Required columns: ticket_id, vendor, priority, opened_at. Blank response or resolution times mean the milestone is still open.

CSV format, scope & privacy

Use ticket_id, vendor, priority, opened_at, responded_at, resolved_at, owner, issue_key. Priorities are P1–P4. Timestamps need a timezone, for example 2026-09-01T09:00:00Z. The manual form uses UTC. Owner and issue key are optional.

All tickets must open on or before the cutoff. Milestones after the cutoff are treated as incomplete at that snapshot. Resolved tickets need a response timestamp. Duplicate vendor/ticket ID pairs and impossible timestamp sequences are rejected.

Match the cutoff and imported period to the review you want to run. Data stays in this tab’s memory and is lost on refresh or close. No analytics, remote scripts, or browser storage are used. Download the rules and ticket CSV before leaving.

Compliance = met checks ÷ (met + breached checks), across response and resolution. Pending checks are excluded. Open milestones past their deadline count as breaches. This is a check-level metric, not the percentage of tickets meeting both targets.

Availability calculator

A separate check for a single service and review period.

Independent of ticket durations
Availability = (period minutes − downtime) ÷ period minutes. Enter non-overlapping downtime counted under your agreement. Ticket resolution time is not assumed to be service downtime.
