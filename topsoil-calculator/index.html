Planning tips

  • Soil settles after placement.
  • Wet soil weighs substantially more.
  • Confirm truck access before delivery.

How many yards of topsoil do I need?

Measure the length and width of the area, then choose the finished soil depth. Convert inches to feet and divide cubic feet by 27.

Cubic yards = length × width × depth (inches) ÷ 324

Common topsoil depths

One to two inches can amend an existing lawn. New lawns often use four to six inches, while vegetable beds may use eight to twelve inches depending on the existing soil.

How much does a yard of topsoil weigh?

A cubic yard can weigh roughly 1 to 1.4 tons depending on composition and water content. Use the supplier's stated weight when checking delivery or vehicle limits.
