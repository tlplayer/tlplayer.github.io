Gravel Coverage Chart

One cubic yard contains 27 cubic feet. Its square-foot coverage depends on the installed depth.

Method reviewed: September 4, 2026

How much area does one cubic yard of gravel cover?

One cubic yard of gravel covers 108 square feet at 3 inches deep, assuming a uniform finished depth. At 2 inches it covers 162 square feet, and at 4 inches it covers 81 square feet.

Coverage (ft²) = 324 ÷ depth (inches)

Formula for a rectangular gravel area

When length and width are measured in feet and depth is measured in inches, divide by 324 to get cubic yards.

Cubic yards = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in) ÷ 324

Assumptions and limits

  • Coverage assumes a level area and a uniform finished depth.
  • The table describes volume, not weight. Gravel type, grading, moisture, and void space change density.
  • Compaction and uneven subgrade can increase the delivered quantity.
  • Confirm supplier order increments and whether quoted material is loose or compacted.
  • For vehicle surfaces, base design depends on soil, drainage, climate, loads, and local practice.
