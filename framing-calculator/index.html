How many studs are in a wall?

Divide wall length by on-center spacing, round up, then add one end stud. This estimator adds a user-selected number of studs for each door or window and applies waste to the final count.

Base studs = round up(wall length ÷ spacing) + 1 + opening studs

Stud spacing

Sixteen-inch and 24-inch on-center layouts are common options, but the correct spacing depends on the approved plans, loads, sheathing, finishes, wall type, and local requirements.

Top and bottom plates

A simple wall is often estimated with one bottom plate and two top-plate runs. Openings, laps, treated bottom plates, intersecting walls, and special assemblies change the material list.

What is not included?

Headers, king and jack stud details, corners, blocking, fire stops, bracing, hold-downs, hardware, and engineered components are not calculated. Use the result as a purchasing estimate, not a framing plan.
