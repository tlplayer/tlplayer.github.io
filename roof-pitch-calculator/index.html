Measure safely

  • Measure from an accessible attic when possible.
  • Keep rise and run in the same units.
  • Do not climb a wet or unsafe roof.

What is roof pitch?

Roof pitch describes vertical rise for every 12 inches of horizontal run. A roof that rises 6 inches over a 12-inch run is a 6:12 roof.

Pitch = (rise ÷ run × 12):12

Pitch to degrees

The roof angle is the arctangent of rise divided by run. A 4:12 pitch is about 18.4 degrees, a 6:12 pitch is about 26.6 degrees, and a 12:12 pitch is 45 degrees.

Using the roof multiplier

Multiply the horizontal footprint area by the pitch multiplier to estimate sloped roof area. This does not include eaves, dormers, valleys, hips, or material waste.
