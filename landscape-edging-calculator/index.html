Landscape Edging Calculator

Estimate edging length, pieces, stakes, waste, and optional cost around a rectangular lawn or bed.

Subtract gates, paths, or open sides

Layout tips

  • Measure the actual edging path.
  • Subtract gates and open transitions.
  • Use closer stakes around tight curves.

How much landscape edging do I need?

For a rectangle, add length and width and multiply by two. Subtract any sides or gaps that will not be edged, then add waste and divide by the product's piece length.

Pieces = ((2 × length + 2 × width − gaps) × (1 + waste)) ÷ piece length

How many edging stakes?

Stake spacing depends on product type and soil. Straight runs usually need fewer stakes than corners and curves. Use the manufacturer's installation directions when supplied.

Measuring curved borders

Lay a flexible tape or rope along the intended path, mark the total, and enter that measurement by modeling a rectangle with equivalent perimeter or by dividing the project into smaller runs.

Common edging materials

Steel, aluminum, plastic, composite, wood, and concrete edging come in different piece lengths and connection systems. Enter the package length rather than assuming the nominal size.
