Mulch Calculator

Find the bulk yards or bag count needed to cover a rectangular garden bed.

Mulch tips

  • Use 2–3 inches for most beds.
  • Keep mulch away from trunks and stems.
  • Measure separate beds, then add totals.

How to calculate mulch

Multiply bed length by width and the mulch depth in feet. Divide cubic feet by 27 for cubic yards, or divide by the volume printed on a bag.

Cubic yards = length × width × (depth in inches ÷ 12) ÷ 27

How deep should mulch be?

Two to three inches is enough for most established beds. Use less when refreshing existing mulch. Overly deep mulch can hold excess moisture against plants and tree trunks.

Bulk mulch or bags?

Bags are convenient for small or hard-to-reach areas. Bulk delivery is often more practical for several cubic yards. Compare delivered price, cleanup, and where the load can be placed.
