Board Foot Calculator

Calculate lumber volume for a single board or multiply it across a full order.

Quick reference

  • One board foot equals 144 cubic inches.
  • Use nominal dimensions for rough lumber.
  • Multiply total by price per board foot.

Board foot formula

A board foot is a volume equal to a board 1 inch thick, 12 inches wide, and 1 foot long. Multiply thickness in inches by width in inches by length in feet, then divide by 12.

Board feet = thickness (in) × width (in) × length (ft) ÷ 12 × quantity

Nominal or actual dimensions?

Hardwood and rough lumber are commonly priced using nominal dimensions before surfacing. If a seller prices finished material by exact volume, enter the measured dimensions instead.

Calculating lumber cost

Multiply the total board feet by the supplier's price per board foot. Add a project-specific waste factor for defects, grain selection, cutting, and joinery.
